About this property

Set behind dual gates at the end of a private drive and spanning approximately 107 acres in the rolling hills of Livermore Valley, this extraordinary estate offers a rare combination of resort-style living, complete privacy, and breathtaking natural beauty just minutes from Blackhawk and the greater Bay Area. Designed by acclaimed architect William Wood and completed in 2008, the single-story residence encompasses approximately 8,144 square feet with 5 bedrooms, 5 full baths, and 2 half baths, thoughtfully positioned to capture panoramic sunrise and sunset views. The private drive winds through vineyards and rolling hillsides before arriving at a dramatic roundabout framed by timeless architecture, mature landscaping, and sweeping views stretching toward Mt. Diablo and beyond. Elevated above the valley below, the estate evokes the atmosphere of a secluded Tuscan retreat while remaining connected to Danville, the East Bay, Silicon Valley, San Francisco, and San Jose. Designed for both entertaining and everyday living, the residence blends warmth, craftsmanship, and scale throughout. Soaring ceilings with exposed wood beams, custom finishes, rich stonework, arched passageways, and expansive windows create interiors that feel both luxurious and inviting. The chef’s kitchen features premium appliances, custom cabinetry, expansive prep areas, and a spacious butler’s pantry flowing seamlessly into the home’s formal and informal living spaces. Additional amenities include a striking interior bar, temperature-controlled wine cellar, office/library, private theater room, and a glass-enclosed in-home car showroom designed to showcase collector vehicles as part of the residence itself. The primary suite serves as a luxurious retreat with tranquil hillside views, dual walk-in closets, and separate his-and-her water closets. Outdoor living was designed to embrace the Northern California lifestyle with a resort-inspired pool and spa, expansive patios, manicured lawns, mature olive trees, bocce court, and outdoor BBQ area overlooking the surrounding hills and vineyard landscape. The property also offers approximately 13 garage spaces across three separate garage structures. Positioned above the valley floor, the estate offers a rare sense of privacy and scale while remaining connected to the heart of the Bay Area. More than a home, this is a true Livermore Valley legacy estate.
